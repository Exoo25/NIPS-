About NIPS

New International Public School & College in Danyore, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan, is a private institution providing quality education from primary to college level. It focuses on strong academic foundations while helping students develop confidence, discipline, and life skills. The school encourages both curricular and extracurricular activities to support overall growth, preparing students for exams, higher education, and future careers.
